Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you join the pack via our Sign Up page, we collect your email address, display name, and optional details about your dog (name, breed, and bio).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your display name and dog details are used to create your public profile within the Community section. Your email is used for account authentication and security via Firebase.

3. Data Visibility

Please be aware that your "Dog Name" and "Dog Breed" will be visible to other registered members in the "Discover the Pack" and "Member Directory" sections to facilitate community interaction.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for authentication and database management. Your data is stored securely according to Firebase's industry-standard protocols.
