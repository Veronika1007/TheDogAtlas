Cookie Policy

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to help the website function correctly.

2. Essential Cookies

With the implementation of our login system, we use essential cookies provided by Firebase Authentication. These cookies allow the site to "remember" that you are logged in as you move between the Map, Planner, and Community sections. Without these, you would have to log in every time you changed pages.

3. Managing Cookies

You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings; however, doing so will prevent you from accessing your Profile, following friends, or posting in the Forum.
